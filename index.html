Butler

Butler is a personal voice assistant that runs on its owner's home server. It is a private, single-user project: it is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission, Butler reads and updates his Google Calendar so he can ask things like "what do I have tomorrow?" or "move my dentist appointment to 4", by voice or from a chat page on his home network. Changes to existing events are only made after he confirms them.
